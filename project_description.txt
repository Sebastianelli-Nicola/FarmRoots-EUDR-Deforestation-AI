Scope?
The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) will enter into force on December 30, 2026 (June 30, 2027 for small businesses). It prohibits the placing or export from the EU market of commodities (wood, cattle, soy, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, rubber) and derived products associated with deforestation after December 31, 2020. Therefore, companies must demonstrate the legal origin of millions of tons of raw material produced by millions of small farmers. Otherwise, they risk penalties of up to 4% of turnover (millions), exclusion from public markets, and significant reputational damage.
Objective (product/service to be created)
To provide a web app that can be integrated with company management systems (ERP), capable of:
monitoring the compliance of suppliers' lands with environmental regulations along the entire supply chain in real time
predicting the risk of deforestation to assess the risk of land lots (and thus for decision support -> which suppliers to maintain and which to change because they are too risky). A low risk entails a lower probability of controls by competent bodies and thus a lower probability of disputes, which may arise since the criteria for risk assessment are qualitative and therefore ambiguous. Furthermore, lower risks mean lower expenses for risk mitigation.
supporting the automated production of due diligence documentation required by European and national regulations

Features
Automatic Due Diligence compilation (must be done annually)
for each product:
plot coordinates (in GEO-JSON format)
supplier
sub-supplier (if present)
quantity
date of production/harvest
land geolocation
point if < 4 hectares
polygon if >= 4 hectares
proof that the lot is deforestation free (the land was NOT deforested after December 31, 2020)
Risk assessment
Following the 14 regulatory criteria
Risk mitigation
Mitigation of risks identified in the risk assessment
Risk prediction based on the most impactful criteria (those that benefit us: e.g., risk of deforestation in adjacent areas, at the country level, …)
Digital signature of the prediction creates a small data package with: land ID, coordinates, date, and the prediction. It signs it with a secret cryptographic key. This package is sent to the Blockchain (e.g., Polygon).
If the outcome is GREEN: An immutable certificate of conformity is generated.
If the outcome is RED: The smart contract blocks the lot. The company receives an immediate alert and the supplier is suspended from the automated supply chain.

Value Proposition:  
Our web app(Farm Roots) empowers companies to navigate the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) with confidence, ensuring compliance, mitigating risks, and protecting their reputation. Providing real time monitoring of supply chains, predictive risk assessments, and automated due diligence documentation. This results in reduced penalties, lower operational costs, and a sustainable, transparent supply chain, all while safeguarding against reputational damage.



Executive Summary

This report outlines a Software as a Service (SaaS) business model designed to help companies comply with the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). The model focuses on generating revenue through tiered subscription fees, add-ons, and potential data services. Sales and marketing strategies include direct sales, partnerships, content marketing, and online advertising. Key selling points emphasize compliance, efficiency, risk mitigation, transparency, and brand reputation. Customer success is prioritized through excellent support, regular communication, and a strong customer community.
